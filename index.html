Oxygen & Vehicle Support Protocol

  • Personal 1.4L Medical Carbon-Fiber Cylinder: 1 bottle gifted per traveler (medical-grade purity, 6–10 hours continuous flow).
  • Portable Spray Oxygen Cans: 2 × 1,000 ml handheld cans per traveler for passes & walking tours.
  • Onboard Coach Reserves: 1 large medical steel cylinder plus oral glucose ampoules carried in the 9–17 seat tour vehicle.
  • Oxygenated Hotels: Diffused & nasal oxygen plus humidifiers in Lhasa (3 nights), smart oxygen & humidifiers in Shigatse (2 nights), and heated oxygen rooms at EBC / Rongbuk.

CDC Yellow Book Acclimatization Checklist

  • Gradual Entry: Fly into Nyingchi (2,900 m) for initial adaptation rather than landing directly in Lhasa (3,650 m).
  • First 48 Hours: Avoid alcohol completely, keep physical exertion mild, and hydrate steadily in dry plateau air.
  • Caffeine & Medication: Regular caffeine drinkers should maintain normal intake to prevent withdrawal headaches; consult a physician regarding acetazolamide prophylaxis.
  • Symptom Rule: Never ascend to sleep at a higher altitude if experiencing headache, nausea, or dizziness.
