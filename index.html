Critical Hardware & -18°C Field Gear Reminder

Mandatory Stability: Pack a heavy-duty tripod or a sturdy selfie-stick with integrated tripod legs & phone clamp. Handheld 10–25 second exposures will blur completely in EBC plateau winds.

Sub-Zero Power & Comfort: At -15°C to -18°C, lithium camera and phone batteries drain rapidly—keep spare cells inside an inner chest pocket with chemical hand warmers. Use a red-light headlamp, dress in windproof down layers, and keep your 1.4L portable oxygen canister accessible.
