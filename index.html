CDC High-Altitude Best Practices

First 48 Hours & Symptom Rules

  • Zero Alcohol First 48h: Avoid alcohol during Days 2–3 while adjusting from 500m to 2900m–3650m.
  • Keep Caffeine Steady: Regular coffee/tea drinkers should maintain normal intake to avoid withdrawal headaches mimicking AMS.
  • Mild Exertion Only: Walk slowly in Nyingchi, Lhasa, and Potala Palace stairs; drink warm water consistently.
  • Medication Note: Consult a physician before departure about acetazolamide prophylaxis and avoid ascending to sleep if unwell.

Milky Way & Cold-Plateau Shooting Plan

On the night of March 29, 2027 at Rongbuk / Everest Base Camp (5200m), dry alpine air and zero urban light pollution deliver razor-sharp Milky Way core visibility over Everest's North Face during the late-night dark window. Note that the April New Moon falls on April 6–7, 2027; on March 29 (~21 days into the lunar cycle), early evening is moon-free before late waning gibbous moonrise, giving a crisp dark window right after astronomical twilight.

Essential Gear Reminder · Do Not Forget Before Packing

Bring a Sturdy Tripod or Smartphone Tripod Mount / Selfie Stick

At 5,200 m with gusts across the Rongbuk valley, 15–20 second exposures cannot be hand-held. Pack a sturdy travel tripod for mirrorless/DSLR cameras, or a smartphone tripod mount / tripod-leg selfie stick for Night Mode and Pro RAW long exposures. Keep spare batteries inside an inner jacket pocket against body heat—temperatures of -15°C to -18°C cut Li-ion battery life by more than half.
